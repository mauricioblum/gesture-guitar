## Design Context

### Users
People playing air guitar in front of a webcam, usually at night, at home, just for fun or to show friends. They stand back from the screen, so the interface has to read from about a meter away while both hands are busy. Controls get touched rarely; the canvas (their own hands plus the virtual guitar) is the product.

### Brand Personality
Loud, warm and handmade. It should feel like plugging a real acoustic into a small Marshall stack in your bedroom: tactile, vintage and a little theatrical. The goal is delight and a sense of "this is a real instrument," not a toy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Marshall MS-2 amp** for all chrome/UI: black tolex texture, cream-white piping, a brushed-gold faceplate with engraved dark condensed caps, knurled gold knobs, a red power LED, a cream script logo on basketweave grille cloth.
- **Sunburst acoustic** (000/OM style) for the playing surface on the canvas: amber-to-black sunburst spruce top, cream binding, rosette, tortoiseshell pickguard, rosewood fretboard with pearl dots, a bone nut/saddle/bridge pins, bronze wound strings.
- The theme is dark because it's played at night and the camera feed sits behind everything, but every surface is a material (tolex, gold, wood) and never a flat gray.
- Anti-references: generic dark dashboards, neon/glow accents, glassmorphism, rainbow degree colors, monospace "tech" UI.

### Design Principles
1. **Every surface is a material.** Tolex, brushed gold, spruce, rosewood, bone. No flat panels.
2. **Controls are amp controls.** Knobs, toggles and an LED, with native inputs underneath for accessibility.
3. **The guitar teaches.** The fretboard shows where the fingers of the current chord would go.
4. **Readable from a meter away.** A big script chord name with few words on screen.
5. **The canvas stays alive.** The hands and strings always come first, and chrome is kept at the edges.
